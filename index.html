        Questions

        What age are the books for?

        Children aged 3 to 8, read together with a grown-up. The pictures hold younger children; the questions in each story give older ones plenty to think about.

        How does the Club work?

        Each month we send a new Little Fox book plus eight things to go with it, including a reflection sheet, a colouring page, a crossword and a Wild Challenge card for outdoors. Boxes ship at the start of each month, with free UK delivery.

        Can I cancel the Club?

        Yes, any time, with no minimum commitment. Cancel before the 1st of the month and your next box won’t be sent.

        How does the Christmas pre-order work?

        We print in London in small runs, so we’ll print the Christmas edition once 250 families have pre-ordered. If we don’t reach that by 10 December, you can choose another Little Fox book instead, or have a full refund.

        Is the Gift box a subscription?

        No. It’s three months of the Club, gift wrapped with a card, paid once. It stops on its own afterwards.

        When will my order arrive?

        Books and gift boxes leave us within 1 to 2 working days and usually arrive in 3 to 5 working days. See our delivery page for the details.

        Do you ship outside the UK?

        Not yet. If you’d like us to ship to your country, email hello@thewildlearningcollective.com and we’ll let you know when we do.

        What makes these different from other picture books?

        Most children’s books rush to the lesson. Little Fox takes his time: the stories sit with a feeling before offering an answer, and often end with a question, so the meaning belongs to the child reading.
