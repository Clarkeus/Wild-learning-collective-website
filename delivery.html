Delivery and returns

Everything you need to know about getting Little Fox to your door.

Where we deliver

We currently deliver within the UK only. UK delivery is free on every order.

Books and gift boxes

Orders leave us within 1 to 2 working days and usually arrive within 3 to 5 working days. We send everything with Royal Mail or a tracked courier.

The Club

Club boxes are sent at the start of each month. If you join partway through a month, your first box goes out straight away.

You can cancel any time. Cancel before the 1st of the month and your next box won’t be sent.

Christmas pre-orders

We print the Christmas edition in London once 250 families have pre-ordered. If we reach that, pre-orders are sent in time for Christmas.

If we don’t reach 250 by 10 December, you can choose another Little Fox book instead, or have a full refund. We’ll email you either way.

Changing your mind

You can cancel an order within 14 days of receiving it, for any reason. Email us, then send the book back unused and in its original condition, and we’ll refund you within 14 days of getting it back. Return postage is paid by you unless the item is faulty.

Get in touch

Email hello@thewildlearningcollective.com and we’ll reply within 2 working days.
