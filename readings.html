Book a reading with Olivia

Olivia is co-author of Little Fox, and she’d love to read the stories aloud with your children, in person.

What happens

Olivia reads a Little Fox story with the children, then leads a gentle conversation about it: how Little Fox felt, what they noticed, what they might have done. Sessions can end with drawing, or with a Wild Challenge outdoors.

Where Olivia reads

  • Nurseries and primary schools
  • Libraries and bookshops
  • Parent and toddler groups
  • Birthday parties and community events
